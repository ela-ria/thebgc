Don't forget

Pack list

If it rains Most of our stops are malls, so we can move indoors. The outdoor photo stops, like the park and the LED screen, are the ones to shorten or skip. Keep an umbrella handy for the walks between stops.
Before we go

Good to know

  • BringWater, a powerbank, and comfortable shoes, and pamaypay or fan. We walk a lot.
  • BudgetFood, fare inside BGC, and the Mind Museum ticket if you join.
  • TimingThe bus leaves at 8:00 AM and the return is around 5 to 6 PM. Please arrive early.
  • Getting aroundLegs over 15 minutes can feel long in the heat. Grab or the BGC bus help; the Mind Museum site lists the BGC Central bus from the Market Market terminal.
