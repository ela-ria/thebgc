Itinerary

The plan

All times are estimates. Post a photo at each stop, then tick it off. Everyone sees it live.

  1. 8:30 AM

    Meet at the terminal

    Saint Rose Transit Terminal, Calamba Crossing. Eat breakfast before you come, buy your ticket, and get in line together.

  2. 9:00 AM

    Bus departs

    Saint Rose P2P to Market Market. About 2 to 2.5 hours depending on traffic.

  3. About 11:30 AM

    Arrive at Market Market

    CR break, quick outfit and makeup retouch, then regroup.

  4. 11:40 AM

    Walk to SM Aura

    Short walk across the street.

    From Market Marketabout 10 min walkDirections
  5. 12:00 to 1:30 PM

    Lunch and photos at SM Aura

    Budget lunch at the food court or a fast food spot, then walk around and take pictures. The Sky Park on the top floor is a good photo spot.

  6. 1:30 PM

    Walk to High Street

    This is the hottest part of the day, so bring your fan and water. Grab is an option if it's too hot.

    From SM Auraabout 1.5 km, 20 min walkDirections
  7. 1:50 to 3:30 PM

    Bonifacio High Street

    The BGC 3D LED Screen, One Bonifacio High Street Park, a walk past the shops, and lots of photos. The whole strip is only about 10 to 15 minutes end to end.

  8. 3:30 PM

    Walk to Uptown Mall

    Leaving early so we're not rushing for the 5:00 PM booking.

    From High Streetabout 15 to 20 min walkDirections
  9. 3:50 to 4:45 PM

    Quick walk around Uptown

    Cool down inside the mall, CR and retouch, then a quick look around before dinner.

  10. 4:45 PM

    Check in at TGI Fridays

    Be at the restaurant a little early so we keep our al fresco table.

  11. 5:00 to 6:15 PM

    Dinner at TGI Fridays Uptown

    Booked for 5:00 PM. The 375 meal, details below. Sunset is around 5:30, so it's a nice time for photos.

  12. 6:15 to 7:45 PM

    Walk around Uptown

    Uptown Mall and Uptown Parade at night. Last chance for photos with the city lights.

  13. 7:45 PM

    Book a Grab

    Book a 4-seater back to Market Market, about 150 per car. Split it four ways.

  14. 8:00 PM

    Leave Uptown

    Saturday night traffic can be slow, so don't book later than this.

    From Uptown Mallabout 10 to 15 min by GrabDirections
  15. 8:30 PM

    At the Market Market terminal

    Buy tickets and line up for the Saint Rose bus back to Calamba.

  16. 9:00 PM

    Bus home

    Back in Calamba Crossing around 10:30 to 11 PM.

    Good to plan for Bring around ₱1,400 so there's a little cushion.
    • Eat breakfast at home before the 9:00 AM bus. It isn't in the budget.
    • Bus fare is an estimate (listed at 180 to 195). Confirm at the Saint Rose ticket counter.
    • Optional add-ons at TGI Fridays: appetizer upgrades 30 to 50, entrée upgrades 50 to 110, drink upgrades 50 to 80, desserts 90.
    • The Grab fare can go up with surge pricing on a Saturday night. If fewer than four ride, the share goes up too.
    • To save more: skip the add-ons and bring your own water.
    Don't forget

    Pack list

    If it rains Most of our stops are malls, so we can move indoors. The outdoor photo stops, like the park and the LED screen, are the ones to shorten or skip. Keep an umbrella handy for the walks between stops.
    Before we go

    Good to know

    • BringWater, a powerbank, and comfortable shoes, and pamaypay or fan. We walk a lot.
    • BudgetBus fare, lunch, the TGI Fridays dinner, and your share of the Grab.
    • TimingMeet at 8:30 AM for the 9:00 AM bus. The bus home leaves Market Market at 9:00 PM. Please be on time.
    • Last tripAsk at the Saint Rose counter in the morning what time the last bus back to Calamba leaves, so we don't miss it.
    • Getting aroundWalking legs are 10 to 20 minutes and can feel long in the heat. Grab is fine if anyone gets tired.
    • DistancesWalking times are estimates. Tap Directions on each stop for the live route.
    • Dinner bookingTGI Fridays Uptown at 5:00 PM. Request an al fresco table when booking, with indoor as the backup if it rains.
